     1→// ============================================================================
     2→// KPIMS — Kosar Production & Inventory Management System
     3→// سامانه برنامه‌ریزی تولید و مدیریت موجودی کوثر
     4→//
     5→// Phase 1 — initial relational schema (PostgreSQL 15+ / Prisma)
     6→//
     7→// Conventions:
     8→//   • Tables/columns are snake_case (via @@map / @map) for ERP-friendly SQL.
     9→//   • Primary keys are UUID v4 (@db.Uuid).
    10→//   • All quantities/weights are Decimal (no floating point for inventory).
    11→//   • Timestamps are stored in UTC (timestamptz); business dates use DATE
    12→//     and are displayed in the Jalali calendar by the application layer.
    13→//   • Document status (workflow) and posting status (accounting effect) are
    14→//     kept separate; posted documents must never be silently edited/deleted
    15→//     (enforced by FK onDelete: Restrict and future service-layer rules).
    16→//   • Inventory transactions form an immutable ledger; corrections are made
    17→//     with reversal documents referencing the original transaction.
    18→// ============================================================================
    19→

    21→  provider = "prisma-client-js"
    22→}

    25→  provider = "postgresql"
